Stride — project instructions

Stride is a personal reasoning agent over a self-logged life: you log inputs (things you do or control) and outcomes (things you observe), and on demand an LLM reasons over SQL-computed statistical sketches + entity descriptions to return grounded, range-scoped insights — not a habit tracker with a hardwired correlation engine.
